A join that cannot be written

A refund knows its payment_id. A chargeback knows an ARN and four digits of a card. A support credit knows whatever the agent typed into a ticket at the time.

These are the same order. No query reaches across them, so nobody at the merchant can answer the only question that matters: how much has this order already paid back?

The bill for guessing

Flagging aggressively feels safe and is not. A wrongly held refund does not merely annoy someone: a share of those customers file the very chargeback the system existed to prevent. A false positive manufactures the loss.

Set the line too low and the bill overtakes everything recovered. The merchant runs the detector and ends up further behind than doing nothing.

Measured against what exists

Held-out orders only, split on order date, with planted confusers for every real duplicate. Precision had to be earned against cases built to look exactly like the thing being hunted.

Hand a naive detector perfect entity resolution and it still reaches 8.1% precision, because most orders touching two channels are entirely legitimate. Linking is only half the problem.
